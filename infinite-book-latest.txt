CHAPTER XXV

SURPRISES FOR ROY


Henning arrived at the Cuthberton depot at seven in the morning. In

"Welcome back, old fellow, to St. Cuthbert's," said Ambrose. "I was
very sorry to hear of your loss. May she rest in peace," and the
