## 1️⃣ What is the Thirukkural?  
- **Author** : Thiruvalluvar (திருவள்ளுவர்) – ஒரு **sage‑poet** from the Sangam era (≈ 1 ம. பி.).  
- **Structure** : 1330 couplets (குறள்) → **133 chapters** → **3 books** (மூன்று பகுதி).  
- **Books** :  
  1. **அறம் (Aram – Virtue / Ethics)** – 38 chapters, 380 couplets.  
  2. **பொருள் (Porul – Wealth / Statecraft)** – 70 chapters, 700 couplets.  
  3. **இன்பம் (Inbam – Love / Pleasure)** – 25 chapters, 250 couplets.  

Each **kural** = 2 lines, **4-8** words per line – super concise, super deep.
