**Basic facts (English‑Tamil mix)**  
- **Name:** Earth (பூமி)  
- **Type:** Terrestrial planet (இரும்பு‑பாறை) in the Solar System.  
- **Diameter:** ~12,742 km (≈7,918 mīl).  
- **Mass:** 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg (≈5.97 sextillion kilograms).  
- **Orbit:** 1 AU (astronomical unit) ≈ 149.6 million km from the Sun; takes 365.25 days → 1 year.  
- **Rotation:** 23 h 56 m (sidereal day); 24 h solar day → day‑night cycle.  

**Atmosphere (வாயு மண்டலம்)**  
- 78 % Nitrogen (N₂)  
- 21 % Oxygen (O₂) – life‑supporting gas.  
- 0.9 % Argon, CO₂, Neon, Helium, Methane, etc.  
- **Greenhouse gases** keep the surface at ~15 °C average (comfort zone).
