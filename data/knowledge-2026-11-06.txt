### 🇮🇳 Overview
India (Bharat) என்பது South‑Asia‑ல் இருக்கும் **7th biggest country** (area‑வோடு) and **2nd most populous** நாடு. 2024‑இல் population **≈ 1.43 billion** people. Capital **New Delhi**, biggest city **Mumbai** (financial hub), plus **Bengaluru**, **Chennai**, **Kolkata**, **Hyderabad**, etc.

### 🌍 Geography
- Area: **≈ 3.287 million km²** (including islands).  
- Borders: Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar + Indian Ocean (Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea) and **Andaman‑Nicobar** & **Lakshadweep** islands.  
- Major rivers: **Ganga**, **Yamuna**, **Brahmaputra**, **Godavari**, **Krishna**, **Kaveri**.  
- Himalayas (north) → Thar Desert (west) → Western & Eastern Ghats (south).

## Internet‑oda Basic Overview  

- **What is it?**  
  Internet‑na, oru **global network** of millions of computers, servers, routers, smartphones etc. Oru **“world‑wide web”** madhiri. Ithu data‑ packets‑a send‑pannuthu, receive‑pannuthu, and we can browse, chat, stream, shop, learn – ellam one click‑la.

- **How does it work?**  
  1. **IP address** – Oru device‑kku unique number (e.g., 192.168.1.5).  
  2. **DNS (Domain Name System)** – “google.com” maathiri human‑readable names‑a IP‑kku convert pannuthu.  
  3. **Packets & Routing** – Data‑kku “small boxes” (packets) a cut‑panni, routers‑la path‑a decide panni, destination‑kku send pannum.  
  4. **Protocols** – TCP/IP, HTTP/HTTPS, FTP, SMTP etc
