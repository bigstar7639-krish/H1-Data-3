PARAGRAPH 121/472 - Bharathiyar Kavithaigal - Mahakavi Bharathiyar

"I can not make up my mind," said Roy, as he inserted the key into the
lock, "whether to recommend the committee to get a wire backstop, or a
canvas one." He had now opened the drawer and was feeling mechanically
for his subscription book. "I think a canvas one will be better because it will not be so hard on
the balls, and be less noisy, too. Why! where is my book--Ah! here it
is." He drew out from the drawer the book containing the list of donors. In
the back of the book Henning had made a rough sketch of what he
supposed was wanted as a pitcher's cage. He showed it to the boys. "Who's the artist?" asked Jack. "Your humble servant," replied Roy. "H'm! Perspective all out. It looks two miles long. I guess the
grease-paint man of last night could do better than that." "That's what you say, Jack," answered Roy good-naturedly; "I would
like to see you do as well, anyway."
