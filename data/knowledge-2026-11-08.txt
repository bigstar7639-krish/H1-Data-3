  **Nandhini** – a beautiful, vengeful woman with a complex past.  
  **Aditya Karikalan** – the charismatic crown‑prince.  
  **Ponniyin Selvan** itself means “the son of the river Kaveri”, i.e., **Arulmozhivarman**.  
- **Plot twists**: conspiracies by the *Pallava* and *Pandya* nobles, secret alliances, hidden letters, and a lot of sword‑and‑strategy drama.
