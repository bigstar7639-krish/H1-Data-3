Knowledge 104 - Ponniyin Selvan - Hey! 😄 Ponniyin Selvan (PS) ennanga? It’s a **historical novel** by the legendary Tamil writer **Kalki Krishnamurthy**, first published serially in *Kalki* magazine between 1950‑1954.  

### Quick rundown  
- **Setting**: 10th‑century Chola empire, during the reign of **Rajaraja Chola I** (Sinnanchiraman).  
- **Main story**: The mystery around the **Prince Arulmozhivarman** (later Rajaraja) – why he’s missing, who’s plotting against the throne, and how the kingdom is saved.  
- **Key characters**  
  - **Vandiyathevan** – a daring, witty warrior‑messenger (our favorite “hero”).  
  - **Kundavai** – Rajaraja’s clever sister, political mastermind.  
  **Nandhini** – a beautiful, vengeful woman with a complex past.  
  **Aditya Karikalan** – the charismatic crown‑prince.  
  **Ponniyin Selvan** itself means “the son of the river Kaveri”, i.e., **Arulmozhivarman**.  
- **Plot twists**: conspiracies by the *Pallava* and *Pandya* nobles, secret alliances, hidden letters, and a lot of sword‑and‑strategy drama.

- **Literary masterpiece** – Kalki blended history, romance, intrigue, and poetic language.
