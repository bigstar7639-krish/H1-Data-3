## 1️⃣ Hinduism (இந்து மதம்)
- **Brahman**: எல்லா existence‑னும் underlying, infinite, formless reality. “அத்மா” (soul) is a part of Brahman.
- **Devas**: Vishnu, Shiva, Devi, Ganesha, etc. – they’re manifestations (avatars) of the same ultimate reality, but people worship them with personal devotion (bhakti).
- **Philosophy**: Advaita (non‑dual) says *“Aham Brahmasmi”* – I am Brahman. Dualist schools (Dvaita, Vishishtadvaita) keep God separate from individual soul.
