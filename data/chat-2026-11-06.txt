### 🇮🇳 Overview
India (Bharat) என்பது South‑Asia‑ல் இருக்கும் **7th biggest country** (area‑வோடு) and **2nd most populous** நாடு. 2024‑இல் population **≈ 1.43 billion** people. Capital **New Delhi**, biggest city **Mumbai** (financial hub), plus **Bengaluru**, **Chennai**, **Kolkata**, **Hyderabad**, etc.

### 🌍 Geography
- Area: **≈ 3.287 million km²** (including islands).  
- Borders: Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar + Indian Ocean (Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea) and **Andaman‑Nicobar** & **Lakshadweep** islands.  
- Major rivers: **Ganga**, **Yamuna**, **Brahmaputra**, **Godavari**, **Krishna**, **Kaveri**.  
- Himalayas (north) → Thar Desert (west) → Western & Eastern Ghats (south).
