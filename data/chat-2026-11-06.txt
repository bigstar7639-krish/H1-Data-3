### 1. **Listen & Understand (Kettu purinjukka)**
- **Active listening**: Oru point‑a solraanga na, adha thavara kaadhalai illaatha **eye contact**-um, **nod**-um seyyunga.  
- **No interrupt**: “Naan solren” nu solra mun, avanga solra maathiri **pause** kudunga.  
- **Feelings‑a validate**: “I understand you’re feeling ___” nu solrathu, avanga emotions‑a acknowledge pannum.

### 2. **Speak Calmly (Mellum pesunga)**
- **“I” statements** use pannunga: *“I feel ___ when ___ happens”* instead of *“You always ___”*.  
- **Avoid blame**: “Why you always…” nu solrathukku badhila, *“Let’s see how we can fix this together”* nu solunga.
