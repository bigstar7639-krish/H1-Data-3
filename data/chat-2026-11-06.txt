- **Length** – Roughly **100,000 ślokas** (couplets) – that’s about **10⁷ words**, making it the longest poem ever composed.  
- **Author** – Traditionally credited to **Vyāsa** (also called *Krishna Dvaipāyana*). He is said to have narrated it to his son *Suka* and later to the world.

- 1,000+ கோடி (billion) devices (phone, laptop, fridge, car)‑கள் ஒன்றோடொன்று **connected** ஆக இருக்குது.

### 2️⃣ History in short
- **1960s**: ARPANET (US defence) → “first packet‑switching network”.

- **1990**: Tim Berners‑Lee created **World Wide Web** (HTML, HTTP).  
- **1995‑2000s**: Commercial ISPs, browsers (Netscape, IE) → “Internet boom”.
