## Internet‑oda Basic Overview  

- **What is it?**  
  Internet‑na, oru **global network** of millions of computers, servers, routers, smartphones etc. Oru **“world‑wide web”** madhiri. Ithu data‑ packets‑a send‑pannuthu, receive‑pannuthu, and we can browse, chat, stream, shop, learn – ellam one click‑la.

- **How does it work?**  
  1. **IP address** – Oru device‑kku unique number (e.g., 192.168.1.5).  
  2. **DNS (Domain Name System)** – “google.com” maathiri human‑readable names‑a IP‑kku convert pannuthu.  
  3. **Packets & Routing** – Data‑kku “small boxes” (packets) a cut‑panni, routers‑la path‑a decide panni, destination‑kku send pannum.  
  4. **Protocols** – TCP/IP, HTTP/HTTPS, FTP, SMTP etc
